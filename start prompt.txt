# DEL 1: ORIGINALUPPGIFTEN FRÅN ERICSSON (ordagrant, källan till sanning)

Campor × BUILD, Hackathon 2026 · 13–15 November · Black Box, Ericsson, Karlskrona
Challenge by Ericsson: Data Storage Optimization

Build a tool that frees up storage without losing information: turn long recordings and documents into compact, searchable, AI-ready data, and find duplicates across systems.

Ericsson's note: this challenge can be solved without sharing sensitive data, and partial solutions can reach the goal. From there, it can grow as big as you like.

## THE CHALLENGE
We want a tool that supports efficient use of data storage, so we can clean out large files but still keep their information at minimum cost. It addresses two issues:
- Storage reduction: replace content such as video with text and key frames, and identify duplicates.
- Data activation: make content searchable and consumable by AI.

The solution can include distilling data, compressing files and identifying duplicate information across systems. In addition, when someone leaves the company, their files should be analysed, compared with existing copies and deleted only if a duplicate already exists.

## WHAT TO PRESENT IN THE FINAL PITCH
Solve the main issues: save storage, make content searchable and feed AI. In your pitch, present:
1. Which use cases you addressed.
2. How you solved the challenges technically.
3. Scenario-based percentage savings in data volume.
4. A possible data strategy that is future-proof and supports a data-driven AI approach.
5. Potential areas for further work, and any problems that remain unresolved.

## BACKGROUND
Ericsson stores and produces a large number of presentations, screen recordings and videos from internal meetings, courses and more. A single recording can be several hours long and often hundreds of megabytes. These recordings take up storage on every platform they live on: SharePoint sites, OneDrive and the third-party platform Ericsson Play.

Because the files are so large, highly informative content is often deleted for lack of storage. Documents on internal sites are also downloaded to laptops and uploaded again to different people's OneDrives, which creates duplicates.

As regulations were enforced, site administrators and owners had to start deleting content from SharePoint sites when they reached maximum storage. Permanent deletion loses all the information in those files, at a time when data is considered the new gold. Deleted files can no longer be used as training data for future AI models. If recordings needed less storage, more of them could be kept for longer, stay accessible to employees and be used to train AI agents and models.
